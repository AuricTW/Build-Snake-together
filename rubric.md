# Lab 03 Snake Trio Rubric - 100 points

| Dimension | Points | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Assignment understanding and four contracts | 15 | Group contract and four falsifiable function contracts completed before implementation. |
| Playable Snake core | 30 | Four functions satisfy the public contract; one round moves, grows, ends, and restarts. |
| Trio-designed tests | 20 | At least three meaningful tests, with each member able to explain one setup and expected result. |
| Debugging evidence | 15 | One genuine red-to-green record uses observed facts and the smallest justified repair. |
| GitHub collaboration | 15 | Private shared repository; every member authors and reviews work; truthful commits and green checks. |
| Demonstration and explain-back | 5 | Trio demonstrates the game; each member independently explains one contract or test. |
| **Total** | **100** | Green tests are necessary but do not automatically earn full credit. |

Undeclared AI use, invented evidence, or one member completing the entire submission is handled under the course integrity policy rather than as an ordinary point deduction.
